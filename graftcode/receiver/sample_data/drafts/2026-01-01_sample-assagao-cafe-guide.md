# Working From a Cafe in Assagao: A Practical Guide

Assagao has a handful of cafes that suit a laptop morning. Expect a quiet room before 10 am, steady wifi and filter coffee that does not rush you out.

## What to bring
Bring a charger and headphones. Few cafes have spare sockets, so arrive early for a wall seat.

## When it gets busy
Weekends fill up by 11 am. Weekday mornings stay calm and are the best time to settle in for a few hours.
